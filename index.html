I’m a security engineer who likes understanding a system end to end: how it gets attacked, how you catch it, and how it recovers. At Seceon, I work in the SOC triaging live alerts, escalating real threats, and helping shape our incident response playbooks, then turn around and harden the product itself. I’ve remediated 30+ critical CVEs and rebuilt our backup and disaster recovery automation across 20+ servers, with restore tests to prove it actually works.

Before that, Tavant taught me that security is as much people and process as it is tooling. I rolled out Qualys, CrowdStrike, Netskope, and Mimecast across 500+ endpoints for vulnerability management, EDR, and email and cloud security, ran phishing training that cut click-through by 18%, and coordinated ISO 27001 audits that closed with zero major nonconformities.

On the offensive side, I train on TryHackMe, PortSwigger, and PentesterLab, and I’m preparing for the OSCP+. Thinking like an attacker makes me a better defender, and it’s how my university team took 1st at the NCAE Cyber Games Northeast regionals, then finished 5th of 90+ teams at nationals. Whether it’s detection and response, cloud security, GRC, or penetration testing, I’m happiest when I understand how something fails and get to make it stronger.
